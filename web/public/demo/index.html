Think like mission planners

Move from orbital observations to an exploration strategy: where to land, where to drive, where to excavate, and how much ice may be available.

Use the Faustini/F2 target model

Frame the candidate as a ~1.1 km doubly shadowed crater in Faustini PSR with lobate-rim morphology, high CPR, and low DOP as the reference evidence pattern.

Separate ice from rocky false positives

Do not treat radar brightness alone as ice. Combine CPR/DOP logic, slope, roughness, morphology, and OHRC hazard evidence.

Deliver five outputs

High-probability target regions, validated radar framework, feasible landing site, optimized traverse path, and a top 0-5 m volume estimate.

Limitations & Scientific Honesty judge-trust guardrails
Candidate ice, not confirmed ice

SCI-B/DSC-1 is a ranked excavation candidate, not a confirmed deposit. SAR-bright and CPR/DOP-like patterns must be treated as evidence-consistent until calibrated CPR/DOP, official AOI registration, and external validation are available.

Exact CPR/DOP is still pending

The dashboard shows the required CPR > 1 and DOP < 0.13 gate and an experimental proxy mask. Exact values still require calibrated polarimetric/MIDAS-style CPR and DOP rasters on the supplied crater AOI.

Official crater AOI is the handoff point

The current DSC-1 polygon is a proxy registration harness. Drop the mentor-supplied crater boundary into data/aoi/official_crater_aoi.geojson to re-run the same pipeline on the official target.

OHRC hazards are AOI-window, not certified

OHRC geometry CSVs now crop hazard candidates inside the active AOI window. Final landing certification still needs map-projected full-resolution OHRC extraction and official AOI intersection.

LOLA validates terrain behavior

NASA PDS LDEM_85S_20M is independent topographic validation for slope/roughness trends. It is not a substitute for local TMC/OHRC landing hazard certification.

Cold-trap and solar are proxy-level

Low-sun hillshade helps screen persistent-shadow and rover-power risk, but final PSR, doubly shadowed crater, and illumination windows need ephemeris-grade polar illumination products.

Top 5 m volume is scenario-based

Volume estimates are area x 5 m x assumed ice fraction. They become defensible only after exact CPR/DOP masks, dielectric/backscatter assumptions, and official crater geometry are locked.

Routes are planning recommendations

The A* traverse ranks safer corridors using terrain, cold-trap interest, and solar penalty. It is not a rover-command path until hazards, slopes, illumination, and localization are certified at mission scale.

Demo Path

  1. Start Demo. Say this is a mission-planning system, not only a visualization.
  2. DSC target. Explain DSC-1 as a Faustini/F2-style proxy until the official crater AOI arrives.
  3. DFSAR evidence. Use the radar layer as candidate evidence, not confirmed ice.
  4. CPR/DOP gate. Call out CPR > 1 and DOP < 0.13 as the real validation target.
  5. Validation gates. Show that raw full-pol DFSAR is staged, official AOI is still pending, and OHRC registration is proxy-ready.
  6. Rough terrain rejection. Show that the model avoids rocky radar false positives.
  7. TMC-2 accessibility. Point to landing safety from slope and terrain relief.
  8. Cold trap and solar. Explain volatile plausibility plus the need for ephemeris validation.
  9. A* traverse. Show the landing-to-science route and solar-power penalty.
  10. Top 5 m volume. Present scenario estimates, not reserves.
  11. LOLA and OHRC. Close with independent terrain validation and optical hazard readiness.

How To Read Metrics

Layer score

Judge-readiness score for that evidence layer. It is not probability of ice.

Confidence ring

Evidence confidence/readiness for the selected layer. Treat it as screening confidence.

Selected Plan

The current action implied by the layer: target, gate, landing zone, traverse, or validation.

Candidate ranking

SCI-B is the science-priority target, LZ-A is the safer landing gate, RIM-C is a review candidate.

Traverse profile

Relative bins for the selected layer. Use it to compare route variation, not as absolute units.

Fusion weights

Prototype ranking weights: radar evidence matters most, but terrain and optical hazards prevent overclaiming.
